Editorial approach

Ideas should invite scrutiny.

Clear attribution

Every essay will name its author, distinguish original publication from republication, and provide links back to earlier editions when known.

Claims in context

Perspective pieces explore possibilities; technical publications document evidence. Neither should be confused with the other.
