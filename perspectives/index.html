Perspectives · Essays & commentary

Beyond Human Discovery.

A connected series exploring machine intelligence, the changing nature of scientific discovery, and what happens when computational capabilities begin to challenge the boundaries of human understanding. These essays develop questions and arguments; they do not report independently verified experimental results, and they do not pretend otherwise.

Featured · Original editorial

What would count as evidence of intelligence?

An argument for observable, repeatable tests of adaptation, retention, planning, recovery and transfer — not claims based on impressive demonstrations alone. The closest thing this institute has to a manifesto, and the seed of its framework.
