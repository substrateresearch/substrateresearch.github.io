Evidence & reproducibility

Independent evaluation matters.

An evaluation is useful when another person can understand what was tested, how it was tested, what passed, and what remains unresolved.

Evaluation standard

We distinguish artifact inspection from execution testing, and a passed test from a broader claim about a system. Reports should identify the tested version, relevant environment, input cases, procedures, measurements and known limitations.

Publication criteria

Independent evaluations appear in the public archive only after their authorship and release status have been confirmed. A technical conclusion is stated as narrowly as the evidence permits.

Reproducibility

Where rights and confidentiality permit, reports will provide checksums, scripts, configurations and links to preserved artifacts. When materials cannot be released, the resulting limitations will be explained.
