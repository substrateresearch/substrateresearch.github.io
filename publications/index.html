Entry 001 — filed, not numbered

The Institute numbers only its own report series. This evaluation was published with Zenodo and keeps its publisher's record; it is filed here as the archive's founding entry under Independent evaluations.

FieldRecord
TitleIndependent Technical Evaluation of Externalized GPT-2 Parameters and Versioned Tensor Interventions
AuthorTony Vidlak
Published1 November 2026 · Version 1.0
SubjectWKClifford LLC — externalized GPT-2 parameter storage and versioned tensor interventions. The evaluated implementation remains the intellectual property of WKClifford LLC.
Permanent recordzenodo.org/records/23873689

What was tested. Whether GPT-2 parameters held outside the model can be materialised faithfully, whether inference on the relocated parameters reproduces the original, whether controlled tensor replacements create recoverable successor states, and how the system behaves when something is wrong.

What was found. All 148 GPT-2 tensors relocated byte-exactly; greedy inference agreed across 320 generated tokens, with a maximum observed absolute logit difference of approximately 2.01 × 10−4; parent and successor states persisted and were retrieved distinctly, with baseline parameters preserved. One limitation was documented, not excused: a successor view referencing a nonexistent replacement artifact failed later than it should have. After correction, the fidelity verification harness passed all specified acceptance conditions and issued a new verification receipt.

What it does not establish. Superior computational efficiency, energy savings, autonomous learning, or general architectural superiority — the report says so itself, in its own abstract, which the Institute considers the correct place for such sentences.

Publication categories

Technical reports describe implementation, experimental method, results and limitations. Independent evaluations document a tested artifact, environment, procedures and reproducibility evidence. Working papers present developing ideas and are identified, in letters of no unusual smallness, as preliminary.

How we identify a record

Each entry includes a title, named author(s), date, version, publication status and durable links. DOI citations will be included when assigned. Numbering is reserved for released reports; numbers are not assigned retroactively to unpublished work, however distinguished the work later becomes. Supporting source code and reproducibility materials will be linked directly from individual publication records when available.

Meanwhile, the laboratory's running record — programmes, verdicts and deaths — is kept current in Projects and the Obituaries.
