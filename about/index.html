About the initiative

A place for careful, independent inquiry.

Substrate Research is a publication and experimental research initiative focused on computational intelligence and the systems that enable adaptive behavior.

Why this exists

Questions about intelligence deserve experiments that can fail, mechanisms that can be inspected, and results that can be revisited. This initiative brings together architecture exploration, engineering prototypes and evaluation reports under a consistent public record.

Substrate Research is independent and does not claim affiliation with a university or established research institution.

Editorial principles

Attribution. Every research publication must identify its actual contributors and authors.

Transparency. Preliminary theories, reproducible findings and external evaluations are clearly distinguished.

Bounded claims. Conclusions must not exceed the evidence supplied.

Confidentiality. Unreleased research, third-party materials and private data remain outside this public website.
