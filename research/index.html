Research agenda

Understanding systems that learn, remember and act.

Our research questions connect computational architecture to observable behavior, with particular attention to persistent state, interactions, and reproducibility.

01

Synthetic & adaptive systems

How can a stateful controller improve its decisions through experience, planning and environmental feedback?

02

Architecture & substrate

What aspects of system organization matter for intelligent behavior beyond a model's parameterization?

03

Agent engineering

How can tool-using systems manage permissions, persist context, detect failures and recover safely?

04

Model fidelity

How do we independently verify tensor bindings, inference behavior and controlled interventions?

05

Experimental methodology

How can a result be reproduced, bounded and distinguished from an attractive but untested interpretation?
