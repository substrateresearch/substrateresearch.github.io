Research agenda

Understanding systems that learn, remember and act.

Our research is organized around three aspects of intelligence—Persist, Adapt and Transfer—posed as questions, with the evidence that would answer them stated in advance. The detailed research questions below sit within that framework.

The framework

Three aspects, posed as questions

01 / PERSIST

Remember what happened

Can a system carry experience forward, so what happened survives a single interaction—in its state, in the environment, or in a record of consequences?

What would count as evidence: later behavior that differs measurably when recorded experience is present versus when it is removed, under otherwise identical conditions.

02 / ADAPT

Change without forgetting

When conditions or rules change, can a system update its behavior—and still retain what was true under the earlier conditions?

What would count as evidence: recovery of performance under the new conditions while performance under the original conditions remains within a bound set before the test.

03 / TRANSFER

Use it somewhere new

Does what was learned hold beyond the situation in which it was learned—new starting points, changed conditions, or a different decision?

What would count as evidence: behavior that remains effective in situations the system was not trained or configured on, without further adjustment.

Research questions

The detailed agenda

01

Synthetic & adaptive systems

How can a stateful controller improve its decisions through experience, planning and environmental feedback?

02

Architecture & substrate

What aspects of system organization matter for intelligent behavior beyond a model's parameterization?

03

Agent engineering

How can tool-using systems manage permissions, persist context, detect failures and recover safely?

04

Model fidelity

How do we independently verify tensor bindings, inference behavior and controlled interventions?

05

Experimental methodology

How can a result be reproduced, bounded and distinguished from an attractive but untested interpretation?
