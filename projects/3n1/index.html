Research programme · Agents and environments

3N1

What changes when a language model acts inside an environment that remembers — even though the model itself is never modified?

Vital statistics

Model under studyAn ordinary language model, unmodified throughout
Variable under testThe environment around the model — state, history, consequences
Experiments to dateA series completed & independently recalculated
Benchmark baselineRun — recency controller is the bar (see front page)
Next milestoneThe agent arms, against that baseline
Current statusActive

Most language-model systems are stateless at their core: each decision is made from whatever context is placed in front of the model at that moment. 3N1 investigates the other half of that arrangement — the persistent environment around the model — and measures what it contributes.

The question

Can persistent environmental state, individual experience histories and recorded consequences influence a system's subsequent decisions — and if they can, by how much, under what conditions, and compared with what simpler explanation?

The programme does not claim learning inside model weights, and it is not a test of consciousness or awareness. The model is held fixed precisely so that any change in behaviour must be explained by what the environment carried forward.

How it is tested

Controlled experiments place tasks in a shared environment; each agent's view is assembled from its role, the environment items it may see, and its own recorded history; and every trial's outcome is stored as that agent's experience for later trials. Comparisons are run against simpler baselines, and reported results are independently recalculated from the underlying records before they are relied on — a precaution the Institute recommends to laboratories with considerably larger budgets.

Keystone

Nothing above is taken on trust. A third presence keeps the record: Keystone, the independent audit log. It records every prompt built, every model request made, every response returned, and every message delivered, each tagged with its experiment, so that any claim about what happened can be checked against the record afterwards.

Keystone sees the environment at every level — the public items, and each agent’s private ones. It never takes part in the work: it sends no message to either agent, and it does not read either agent’s memory. Each agent’s memory is private to that agent; neither agent can read the other’s, and neither can read Keystone’s own record. The arrangement was checked before it was relied on — a dedicated infrastructure validation (E005/P0) ran twenty-four checks and passed all twenty-four, including that separation.

Where it sits in the triad

Persist is 3N1's primary aspect: a direct investigation of what a persistent record of experience does to later behaviour. Adapt is measured by whether those later decisions actually improve with recorded feedback, rather than merely changing. Transfer is tested when a history built in one set of situations is brought to bear on a later, different decision.

Status, stated plainly

A series of experiments has been completed and independently checked. In the programme's controlled benchmark — sixty trials, an unannounced reversal at the halfway point — the scripted baseline controllers have now run: the recency controller the agents must beat posted a median stabilisation at trial 41.5 over the runs that stabilised, with a post-change mean reward of 1.46, while failing to stabilise at all in six of its ten runs. The agent arms — the full loop, recorded experience and all — run against exactly that bar. The Institute will publish the outcome in the usual place: here, regardless.

The benchmark is playable: take it yourself — sixty trials, three levers, one unannounced reversal.

The arms

Axiom. The first arm to run. Five runs, and in all five it chose the safe action on every trial — three hundred decisions, no exceptions. Post-change mean reward 1.00, against the 1.46 the bar required. It never stabilised, and missed on both measures. The full account is in the report.

Vela. The second arm. It has not completed a run. Its evidence database developed a fault the Institute has not yet explained, and its rerun is pending. Until it runs, the milestone stands open.
