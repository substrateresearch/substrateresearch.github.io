Research program · Agents and environments

3N1

What changes when a language model acts inside an environment that remembers—even though the model itself is never modified?

Most language-model systems are stateless at their core: each decision is made from whatever context is placed in front of the model at that moment. 3N1 investigates the other half of that arrangement—the persistent environment around the model—and measures what it contributes.

The question

Can persistent environmental state, individual experience histories and recorded consequences influence a system's subsequent decisions—and if they can, by how much, under what conditions, and compared with what simpler explanation?

The program does not claim learning inside model weights, and it is not a test of consciousness or awareness. The model is held fixed precisely so that any change in behavior must be explained by what the environment carried forward.

How it is tested

Controlled experiments place tasks in a shared environment, assemble each agent's view from its role, the environment items it may see and its own recorded history, and store every trial's outcome as that agent's experience for later trials. Comparisons are run against simpler baselines, and reported results are independently recalculated from the underlying records before they are relied on.

Where it sits in the triad

Persist is 3N1's primary aspect: it is a direct investigation of what a persistent record of experience does to later behavior. Adapt is measured by whether those later decisions actually improve with recorded feedback, rather than merely changing. Transfer is tested when a history built in one set of situations is brought to bear on a later, different decision.

Status

Active. A series of experiments has been completed and independently checked. A controlled benchmark comparing adaptation from recorded experience against a simpler recency-based controller has been prepared and has not yet been run; it is the program's next milestone.

Release status: This page describes the program's question, test design and bounded status. Detailed results, parameters and methods will be published only after review for release, in the publication archive. Nothing here is a claim of general intelligence.
