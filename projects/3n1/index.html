Research programme · Agents and environments

3N1

What changes when a language model acts inside an environment that remembers — even though the model itself is never modified?

Vital statistics

Model under studyAn ordinary language model, unmodified throughout
Variable under testThe environment around the model — state, history, consequences
Experiments to dateA series completed & independently recalculated
Next milestoneControlled benchmark — prepared, not yet run
Current statusActive

Most language-model systems are stateless at their core: each decision is made from whatever context is placed in front of the model at that moment. 3N1 investigates the other half of that arrangement — the persistent environment around the model — and measures what it contributes.

The question

Can persistent environmental state, individual experience histories and recorded consequences influence a system's subsequent decisions — and if they can, by how much, under what conditions, and compared with what simpler explanation?

The programme does not claim learning inside model weights, and it is not a test of consciousness or awareness. The model is held fixed precisely so that any change in behaviour must be explained by what the environment carried forward.

How it is tested

Controlled experiments place tasks in a shared environment; each agent's view is assembled from its role, the environment items it may see, and its own recorded history; and every trial's outcome is stored as that agent's experience for later trials. Comparisons are run against simpler baselines, and reported results are independently recalculated from the underlying records before they are relied on — a precaution the Institute recommends to laboratories with considerably larger budgets.

Where it sits in the triad

Persist is 3N1's primary aspect: a direct investigation of what a persistent record of experience does to later behaviour. Adapt is measured by whether those later decisions actually improve with recorded feedback, rather than merely changing. Transfer is tested when a history built in one set of situations is brought to bear on a later, different decision.

Status, stated plainly

A series of experiments has been completed and independently checked. A controlled benchmark comparing adaptation from recorded experience against a simpler recency-based controller has been prepared and has not yet been run; it is the programme's next milestone, and it is currently open for predictions. The Institute will publish the outcome in the usual place: here, regardless.

Release status: This page describes the programme's question, test design and bounded status. Detailed results, parameters and methods are published only after review for release, in the publication archive. Nothing here is a claim of general intelligence.
