Research programme · Adaptive systems

Emerson

A small, trainable world model that learns how an environment responds to its actions — built to test whether adaptation can grow from interaction, rather than be assumed from scale.

Vital statistics

Kind of systemTrainable world model — not a language model, not pretrained
Prior knowledge of its environmentNone
Training dataIts own interactions, exclusively
Configurations to clear their full bar1 — in a simple environment
Mechanisms discontinued4 (Obituaries)
Current statusExploratory

Emerson begins knowing nothing about its environment. Everything it comes to predict is learned from the consequences of its own actions, inside that environment.

The question

Can a system that starts from nothing learn how its world behaves, act on what it has learned, and then adapt when the rules change — without losing what was true before? And does what it learned in one situation still hold in situations it has never encountered?

How it is tested

Each version is judged in three stages, against criteria set before the run. First, prediction: can it forecast the effects of actions in situations held back from its training? Second, adaptation and retention: when a rule of the environment is reversed, does its behaviour recover — and does it still perform under the original rule afterwards? Third, transfer: does it remain effective from starting points it has never seen?

Approaches that miss their criteria are discontinued, and the outcome is recorded as a result in its own right. A failed mechanism, clearly bounded, is evidence — not an embarrassment to be edited out. The register of the fallen is public: see the Obituaries.

Where it sits in the triad

Persist. The system carries a state updated by every interaction; nothing is learned from a single isolated example. Adapt. Adaptation with retention is Emerson's central test — changing is easy; changing without forgetting is the claim under examination. Transfer. Performance from unseen starting points decides whether what was learned is a model of the environment, or merely a memory of particular episodes.

Status, stated plainly

One model configuration has met its full set of criteria in a simple environment. In more complex environments, results are mixed, and several candidate mechanisms have been tested and discontinued under the criteria above. The next step is a harder environment — not a larger version of a result that already passed, and not a press release.

Release status: This page describes the programme's question, test design and bounded status. Detailed results, parameters and methods are published only after review for release, in the publication archive. Nothing here is a claim of general intelligence.
