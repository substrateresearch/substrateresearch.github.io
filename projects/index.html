Projects · Instruments of investigation

Projects built to test ideas.

Research prototypes are instruments, not products. A project's description states what it is for and where it honestly stands. It does not imply that a hypothesis has been established — establishing things is the experiments' job, and they are frequently uncooperative.

Research programme

Emerson

An exploration of persistent-state, model-based adaptive control: a small system that learns how its environment behaves from its own interaction, and is then examined on prediction, adaptation-with-retention, and transfer. Detailed results are published only after review for release. Its discontinued mechanisms are remembered in the Obituaries.

Research programme

3N1

An investigation of whether persistent environmental state, experience histories and recorded consequences influence a language model's subsequent decisions — without modifying the model. Its next benchmark is currently open for predictions on the front page.

Engineering direction

Interactive agent systems

Practical agent infrastructure for tool execution, state retention, permissions and failure recovery — the unglamorous plumbing on which every grand claim about agents quietly depends.

Evaluation methods

Model & artifact testing

Independent procedures for assessing artifact integrity, execution fidelity and intervention effects. See Evaluations for the standard, and for the Institute's specimen verdict on one of its own systems.
